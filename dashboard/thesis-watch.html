Autonomous Alpha Generation via Multi-Agent LLM Architecture

This dashboard follows a virtual portfolio whose investment decisions are made by AI. Separate research roles examine macroeconomic data, fundamentals and news; their assessments inform the decision-maker and can be incomplete or wrong.

During US exchange sessions, a scheduled detector checks prices and news approximately every 30 minutes. Confirmed thresholds or supported news contradictions trigger reassessment. The decision may be HOLD, BUY, SELL or TRIM; execution validates the session, price timestamps and arithmetic. Delayed data, scheduling delays and model failures can postpone a decision.

Measurement · deterministic

Price, daily change, the 50-day average and the sector ETF arrive live from the connector. The threshold comparison happens here, in your browser — no model commentary touches it.

Commentary · generative

The thesis sentences come from the repository's theses.json, stamped with the round that wrote them. The answer to "what is the status right now?" is not baked into this page; it is produced fresh every time.

Limits · honesty

An unmeasurable condition says unmeasurable; it is never guessed. Hysteresis and cooldown are not on this page — that memory lives in the repository's state/violations.json.
