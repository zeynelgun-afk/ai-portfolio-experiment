A thesis is written together with the number that would refute it

In this experiment the AI makes the decisions and writes down every move. The problem was never the decisions — it was the commentary going stale. The sentence written on Saturday, "+14.9% above the 50-day average", was still sitting in the file on Monday after the price had slipped below that average. A system that thinks once a week is not wrong once a week; it is wrong for six days.

The fix is to tie every thesis to a measurable validity condition. The conditions below are compared against the live price; the commentary is not produced by this page — when you ask, it asks the agent.

Measurement · deterministic

Price, daily change, the 50-day average and the sector ETF arrive live from the connector. The threshold comparison happens here, in your browser — no model commentary touches it.

Commentary · generative

The thesis sentences come from the repository's theses.json, stamped with the round that wrote them. The answer to "what is the status right now?" is not baked into this page; it is produced fresh every time.

Limits · honesty

An unmeasurable condition says unmeasurable; it is never guessed. Hysteresis and cooldown are not on this page — that memory lives in the repository's state/violations.json.

What this page does not show

  • No hysteresis and no cooldown. In the repository, detector.py requires 2 consecutive checks before a condition counts as breached, and it will not rewrite the same claim twice within 4 hours. What you see here is a single instantaneous measurement — a "breach" pill is not the detector's confirmed breach.
  • The volume ratio cannot be measured. The live connector gives today's volume but not the 20-day average, so that condition is marked unmeasurable.
  • This page does not trade. The intraday trading decision belongs to the chain in the repository (detector.py → reassess.py → execute_trade.py), and every step of it is written to DECISION_LOG.md.
  • This is not investment advice. A virtual portfolio, an experiment on paper.
